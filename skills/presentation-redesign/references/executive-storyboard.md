# Executive Strategy Deck Storyboard

Use this reference when a dense roadmap or planning document must become a
live-presented strategy deck. It is an editorial contract, not a slide template.

## Default 16-slide arc

| Slides | Question answered | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What is this deck about? | Dark cover. One strategic statement. |
| 2 | What changed in the world? | Tension. One clear problem and a simple flow. |
| 3 | Why does the product have leverage? | Strategic role. One claim plus three consequences. |
| 4 | What is the operating model? | Four-pillar map. Names and one-line roles only. |
| 5–6 | How do we change workflows? | Pillar transition, then three concrete moves. |
| 7–8 | How do agents become grounded? | Pillar transition, then data-to-answer flow. |
| 9–10 | Where does the product act for the customer? | Pillar transition, then managed-agent loop. |
| 11–12 | How does expertise reach every developer? | Pillar transition, then skills/plugins split. |
| 13 | How do the pillars compound? | One system diagram with named enablers. |
| 14 | What ships in what order? | Three-phase timeline. |
| 15 | What changes for the customer? | Four observable outcomes. |
| 16 | What is the portfolio test? | Closing criterion, not a generic thank-you slide. |

## Headline test

Every slide headline should work as a sentence in this summary:

> Agentic delivery is outrunning platform operations. Terraform is the control
> point. The strategy makes workflow faster, data grounded, help managed, and
> expertise portable. The work compounds in a deliberate sequence and produces
> four customer-visible changes.

If a headline cannot appear in that summary, it is probably a category label or
reference detail, not a live-deck slide.

## Content split

| Put in the live deck | Put in the companion roadmap |
| --- | --- |
| Strategic tension | Full JTBD wording |
| Pillar headline | Every outcome measure |
| Three named moves | Owners and timelines |
| Customer-visible consequence | Exhaustive initiative inventory |
| Sequencing | Detailed project descriptions |

## Review questions

1. Can the audience repeat the four pillars after Slide 4?
2. Does each pillar answer a different customer or platform constraint?
3. Does every diagram clarify a relationship rather than decorate whitespace?
4. Are the phase labels in the roadmap based on source material?
5. Does the final slide give the group a clear test for future investments?

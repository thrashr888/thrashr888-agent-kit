# Brand voice cheat sheet

Voices to offer in Stage 2, with what each is FOR. Present 3–5 per finding,
matched to the audience; let the owner pick per row.

| Voice | Character | Best for | Example shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google | Plain declarative benefit, numbers inline, warm-neutral, no fragments | Body paragraphs explaining results | "The built-in engine scores 0.737 on SciFact, higher than BM25 (0.665), with everything running on your Mac." |
| Vercel | Clipped fragments, stat-forward, imperative | Table cells, chips, micro-copy — not prose | "Declines often. Perfect when it commits." |
| HashiCorp | Sober, spec-like, zero personality | Methodology, claims, footnotes — anywhere credibility is on the line | "Each model is evaluated against the full pipeline: retrieval, citation of the known-correct evidence, and per-claim support." |
| Apple | Two beats and a period; benefit as headline | Headlines and card titles only | "Search, benchmarked." |
| Linear | Quiet confidence, product-as-craft, restrained | Intros and positioning for design-literate audiences | "We test models on what matters in practice: whether the right source gets cited." |
| Stripe | Measured technical precision, elegant qualifiers | Developer-facing claims with numbers | "Benchmarked continuously on BEIR; every run reproduces from the repository." |
| Tailscale | Dry candor, engineering honesty, light wit | Notes columns, caveats, failure admissions | "Great at finding sources, a little too excited about what they say." |

Selection patterns observed in practice:

- Owners rarely pick one voice for everything. The pick pattern usually
  maps registers to surfaces: Apple for headlines, Google for prose,
  HashiCorp where trust matters, Vercel in dense UI.
- Rejections are as informative as picks. Rejecting Vercel for a paragraph
  means fragments are a micro-surface tool in this house; rejecting
  Tailscale means personality stays out of public copy.
- When the owner picks the least charming option for methodology, they are
  telling you credibility outranks voice there. Write WRITING.md
  accordingly.
